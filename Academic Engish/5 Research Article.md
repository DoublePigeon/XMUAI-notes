# IMRD Pattern

(General -> Specific -> Specific -> General)
Introduction -> Methods -> Results -> Discussion

## Introduction

Contexualize Research. This is like establishing conversations between author and other 
experts in the field, and this is done by citations and quotes.

A main purpose of the intro is provide rationale for the article. Which basically what's the reason for your research

Introduction goes from general to specific. In this sense, we **do not** start with our purpose, which is left to the end of our introduction. Instead, we start by buiding a context.

It is called CARS *(Creating a Research Space)* model. We first establish research territory, then establish a niche, then occupy the niche. That is to say, 

"What exists already? 

What's missing?(indicating gap or extending previous knowledge) 
A. counter-claiming (something is wrong) 
B. indicating a gap (something is missing) 
C. raising a question or making an inference (something is unclear) (Kwan and Chan, 2011) D. continuing a tradition (adding something)

How will my research fill the gap?(outlingineg purposes or stating the nature of the present research)"
